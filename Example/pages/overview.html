HCP Morocco Socioeconomic Intelligence Platform

This environment acts as the centralized execution plane for the normalization, cross-survey imputation, and poverty metric modeling workflows mapping the High Commission for Planning (HCP) structural assets. The pipeline anchors consumer behaviors from the National Household Consumption Survey (ENCDM) directly into the decennial General Population and Housing Census (RGPH) microdata layers.

By linking granular consumption indices to broad structural demographics, the engine models validation weights, maps structural poverty indices, and handles localized welfare distribution data streams without compromising structural variables.
